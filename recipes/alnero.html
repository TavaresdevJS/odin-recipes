Pasta Al Nero Recipe

The famous squid ink pasta

Pasta al nero is associated with coastal regions like Veneto. It's a traditional dish and a must-try if you're around Venice for vacation. Just be sure to not be wear white when you eat it!

Ingredients

  • 300g spaghetti or linguine
  • 2-3 squid or cuttlefish with inksacs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/4 cup white wine
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
  • Fresh parsley
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water.
  2. Slice the squid into rings and set aside.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan. Sauté garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add the squid and cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Pour in white wine and let it reduce.
  6. Add the squid ink (squeeze it from the sacs) and stir well. Optionally, add cherry tomatoes for extra flavor.
  7. Toss the pasta into the sauce, adding reserved pasta water if needed.
  8. Season with salt and pepper, garnish with parsley, and serve.
