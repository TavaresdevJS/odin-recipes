Udon Recipe

A traditional japanese dish

A thick noodle made from wheat flour, used in Japanese cuisine. There are a variety of ways it is prepared and served. Its simplest form is in a soup as kake udon with a mild broth called kakejiru made from dashi, soy sauce, and mirin.

Ingredients

  • Fresh or dried udon noodles
  • 4 cups dashi
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Fish cake slices
  • Seaweed

Steps

  1. Boil udon noodles according to package instructions, then rinse under cold water.
  2. In a pot, combine dashi, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar. Heat until simmering.
  3. Place noodles in a bowl, pour the hot broth over them, and add your desired toppings.
  4. Garnish with scallions and enjoy hot.
